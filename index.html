Project 04 · D2C Ecommerce

D2C Ecommerce — Non-Branded Growth & CRO

US Market — Ecommerce SEO · Product & Category Optimization · Technical SEO · CRO · Merchant Center

Challenge

The brand had an established US presence, but Google Search Console showed search visibility concentrated around branded queries, with limited rankings for non-branded keywords. The goal was to attract US mothers searching for breast pumps and convert more visitors into paying customers.

Strategy & Execution
  • Identified high-intent, non-branded keywords with achievable ranking opportunities.
  • Mapped wearable, electric, manual and double breast pump keywords to relevant existing pages.
  • Optimized product and category titles, meta descriptions and H1s for non-branded searches.
  • Updated category content to address purchase needs such as portability, hands-free pumping and returning to work.
  • Refreshed product descriptions with clear benefits, specifications and package contents.
Show all 18 execution steps
  • Fixed indexing restrictions and incorrect canonical tags on priority product and category pages.
  • Added contextual internal links from high-traffic blogs to relevant products and categories.
  • Added concise product FAQs addressing fit, cleaning, battery life, compatibility and warranty.
  • Implemented Product structured data with accurate prices, availability and genuine customer ratings.
  • Optimized Google Merchant Center free listings and corrected product feed inconsistencies.
  • Compressed product images and removed unnecessary scripts to improve mobile loading speed.
  • Improved mobile product layouts to display pricing, benefits, ratings and purchase buttons prominently.
  • Added a sticky mobile “Add to Cart” button to simplify purchasing.
  • Added flange sizing guidance and clarified included sizes beside product selection options.
  • Created a simple pump comparison table to help mothers choose the right model.
  • Added short product demonstration videos and verified customer reviews to support purchase decisions.
  • Simplified checkout with prominent guest checkout, fewer fields and US-friendly payment options.
  • Tracked product-to-checkout drop-offs and fixed friction on high-traffic purchase journeys.

Project 05 · Education · SEO + Meta Ads

School Admissions — Lead Generation with SEO & Meta Ads

Hyderabad — Local SEO · Meta Ads · Landing Pages · CRO · Google Business Profile

Challenge

The client struggled to generate admission enquiries for four schools across Hyderabad. The goal was to reach local parents, build trust by showcasing school facilities, track records and parent testimonials, and convert enquiries into admissions.

Strategy & Execution
  • Designed SEO-optimized admission landing pages with school highlights, facilities, testimonials and clear enquiry forms.
  • Launched Meta Ads with compelling ad copy, relevant parent-focused audiences and school-specific budgets.
  • Configured Hotjar heatmaps and session recordings to identify user friction and improve landing-page engagement.
  • Moved high-interest sections and admission CTAs higher on landing pages based on observed user behaviour.
  • Optimized school websites with relevant keywords, page titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links.
Show all 11 execution steps
  • Added video and text testimonials from parents to homepages and admission landing pages.
  • Optimized Google Business Profiles for all four schools to improve visibility in local searches and Google Maps.
  • Targeted location-specific school and admission keywords for each campus’s surrounding neighbourhoods.
  • Simplified enquiry forms and added click-to-call and WhatsApp options for easier parent enquiries.
  • Retargeted website visitors and engaged Meta audiences with testimonials and campus-visit messaging.
  • Tracked enquiries by campaign and school, shifting budgets toward sources generating qualified admission leads.
